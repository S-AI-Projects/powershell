# Hints

## General

- A year can be even without being a multiple of four. If your function reports 1970 as a leap year, look at your first condition.
- Compare what "divisible by 2" accepts with what "divisible by 4" accepts. Only one of them matches the first rule in the instructions.
- Try these inputs and compare with the table.

| Year | Expected result |
|------|-----------------|
| 2 | `False` |
| 1970 | `False` |
| 1972 | `True` |
